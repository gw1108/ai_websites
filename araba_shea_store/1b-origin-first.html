Who we are

A small business named after a little girl.

Araba’s Shea began with a father from Ghana who couldn’t find honest products for his family’s hair abroad. So he went home for them — to the markets, the soap-makers, the shea traditions his own big family grew up on.

He named the company after his daughter, Araba. Every jar is the promise he made her: real, raw and traditional — the way it’s always been made.
