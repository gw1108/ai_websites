Questions, answered

Is it really 100% raw?

Yes. Our shea butter and black soap are handmade in Ghana the traditional way; the batana oil is pure and unrefined. One ingredient each — nothing else on the label.

Which product should I start with for 4C hair growth?

Start with batana oil massaged into the scalp 2–4× weekly, and seal your ends with shea butter. Consistency over 8–12 weeks is what makes the difference.

How do orders and shipping work?

All orders are fulfilled through Amazon — Prime shipping, Amazon returns and buyer protection, with every purchase supporting our small family business.

What does batana oil smell and feel like?

Earthy and lightly roasted — the natural scent of a truly unrefined oil. It’s a rich, buttery paste that melts on contact with warm skin.
